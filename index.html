POSTDOCTORAL RESEARCHER · UT SOUTHWESTERN

Chen Chang, Ph.D.

CRISPR Technology Development · Functional Genomics · Genome Engineering

I am a postdoctoral researcher at UT Southwestern Medical Center with 10 years of experience developing CRISPR gene-editing technologies. My research focuses on building programmable tools to investigate gene regulation, uncover genetic interactions, and improve precision cell engineering.

I am particularly interested in combining combinatorial CRISPR perturbations, single-cell genomics, and immune cell engineering to develop technologies that reveal and control cellular behavior.

Research

Developing new CRISPR technologies to understand biology and enable precise cellular engineering.

01

Combinatorial CRISPR Perturbation

Developed PAIR, a bidirectional CRISPR platform for simultaneous gene activation and repression, and PAIR-seq for pooled investigation of genetic interactions.

02

Precision Genome Editing

Used large-scale perturbation screening to identify DNA repair network interactions that favor homology-directed repair and support non-viral T-cell engineering.

03

RNA Epigenetic Editing

Developed programmable Cas13-based tools to install or remove m6A modifications on specific RNA transcripts in mammalian cells.
